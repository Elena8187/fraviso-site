Why It Matters

This isn't about the photographer.

Buyers and renters shop with their eyes first. They scroll the photos, picture themselves in the space, and only then decide whether a viewing is worth their time. Weak photos mean fewer views, a longer time on market and a lower perceived asking price. Strong ones change that math.
